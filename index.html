The problem

Reduce Coater Access to Minimize Food Waste

Hey guys! My name is Kiki Rodriguez and I am with CFA Columbus National in Columbus, IN! Just wanted to share an update- after diving in to the root cause of why my store has so much coater waste, I found that the breaders had too much access to the coater. Therefore, I limited their access by only allowing them to have 4 bags of coater at a time and we were able to reduce our food waste cost by $200 that first week the process was implemented! This program is helping me out so much. Looking forward to hear from you guys!
